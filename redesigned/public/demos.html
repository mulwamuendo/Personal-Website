Each demo is a simplified version of a system I build for clients. Everything runs in your browser using made-up businesses and data, so click around freely.

Demo 1 · Customer service

Answer common enquiries instantly, day or night

Customers message at all hours and expect an answer within minutes. This system reads each message, finds the right answer from the business's own FAQ and drafts a friendly reply. Anything it can't answer goes to a person.

Replaces: typing the same answers on WhatsApp and email all day.

Under the hood

This demo uses simple keyword matching so you can see the logic. Steps:

  1. Lowercase the message and split it into words.
  2. Score each FAQ topic by how many of its keywords appear.
  3. Pick the highest score. If it's zero, hand over to a human.
  4. Fill the matched answer into a reply template.

A client version would use an AI model through an API (called from Python) so it understands meaning, not just keywords, and connects to WhatsApp Business.

Demo 2 · Bookings

Let customers book themselves and never miss an appointment

Customers pick a free slot, the calendar blocks it so no one can double-book, and reminders go out automatically before the visit. Fewer no-shows, fewer back-and-forth messages.

Replaces: phone calls to arrange times and manual reminder messages.

Under the hood

Behind the scenes there are three parts:

  1. A list of slots, each marked free or taken.
  2. When a slot is chosen, it is saved and marked taken straight away (this prevents double-booking).
  3. A scheduler calculates send times (24 hours and 2 hours before) and queues the messages.

A client version stores bookings in a database and sends real messages through WhatsApp or SMS, run on a schedule by a Python script.

Demo 3 · Reporting

Turn a messy sales sheet into a clear weekly summary

Paste your sales data and get totals, your best products, your best day and how this week compares with last week, plus a short message you can send to your team. The same report can be emailed automatically every Monday.

Replaces: copying numbers between sheets and writing the summary by hand.

Under the hood

The report is plain arithmetic on a table:

  1. Read each line and split it into date, product, units and price.
  2. Group the rows by day and by product, adding up revenue.
  3. Compare the last 7 days with the 7 before.
  4. Draw the chart and write the summary sentence from the results.

A client version reads the real spreadsheet (Excel, Google Sheets or a database) with Python and emails the report on a schedule.
